01

Where your data lives

Loadout is built to work entirely on your device. Every workout you log, every body-weight entry, every routine you build, and every setting you change is written to storage on your phone and nowhere else. There's no server behind the app collecting or syncing this — there isn't a server at all.

This includes the exact things the app remembers: workout history, active sessions, routines and their weekly schedule, weigh-ins, custom exercises you create, and your app preferences (units, theme, rest-timer length, and similar).

02

What we don't collect

Loadout doesn't ask for an account, an email address, or your name to use the app. It doesn't run analytics or crash-reporting that phones home, doesn't request your location, doesn't touch your contacts, camera, or microphone, and doesn't include advertising of any kind — so there's no advertising ID to collect either.

  • No sign-up, no login, no profile
  • No usage analytics or telemetry
  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No access to contacts, camera, microphone, or location
03

Backups you export

Settings includes an Export backup option that writes your data to a JSON file. Creating and sharing that file is something you choose to do, through Android's own share sheet — Loadout itself never uploads it anywhere. Where that file goes after that (a cloud drive, an email, a folder) is entirely up to you and governed by whatever app or service you send it to, not by Loadout.

04

Loadout Premium (subscription)

The optional monthly subscription is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. When you subscribe, Google handles your payment method and transaction directly — Loadout never sees your card number or any payment details, and doesn't store them. The app only ever receives a simple active-or-not status for your subscription from Google Play's on-device billing library, so it knows whether to unlock the paid content.

Managing or cancelling the subscription happens inside Google Play, not inside Loadout — Settings links straight there.
05

Exercise demo videos

Exercise entries can link out to a YouTube video demonstrating that movement. Tapping one opens YouTube (the app or a browser) outside of Loadout. That's Google's product at that point, governed by YouTube's own privacy policy — Loadout doesn't pass it anything about you.

06

Children's privacy

Loadout isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone — which, per the sections above, is by design true for every user regardless of age.

07

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the Effective date at the top will change with it. Meaningful changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
